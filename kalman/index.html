View

The rocket's true motion is hidden from the navigator. A biased, noisy accelerometer is integrated forward every 10 ms (predict), which drifts; a GPS fix a few times a second pulls the estimate back (update). The Kalman filter weighs the two by how much it trusts each, and carries a covariance that says how wrong it expects to be.
