On Doing Hard Things

A short note on ambition, and why I pick the harder path.

Tiernan Lindauer1 min readOct 30, 2025

Contents

    Most people make grand, vague statements about changing the world during interviews. Very few act like they mean it. I'd rather be the person who does.

    So I try to take the hardest sensible option in front of me: the extra degree, the harder math, the project that could fail publicly. Difficulty is a filter — it selects for the people who keep going.

    Why harder is often safer

    Easy paths are crowded. The hard problems tend to be hard for a reason, which means fewer people are competing on them, and the ones who do are usually worth knowing.

    Never be discouraged. If I were sunk in the lowest pits of Nova Scotia, with the Rocky Mountains piled on me, I would hang on, exercise faith, and keep up good courage; and I would come out on top.

    — Joseph Smith Jr.
